# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Schoolio to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone who
contributes — regardless of experience level, background, identity, or how
they found their way here. We pledge to make participation in this project a
respectful experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in comments, issues, and pull requests.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull
requests, discussions, and commits — and in any other setting when someone
is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it by emailing
**liam.shafferiscool@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and
we'll respond as promptly as we can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate it. Depending on severity, consequences can
range from a warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
